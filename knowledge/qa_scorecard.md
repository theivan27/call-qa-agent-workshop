# Northwind Bank contact center QA scorecard (sample)

Used by Contoso BPO QA analysts for all Northwind Bank voice calls. Total score is out of 100.
Target: 80 or higher. Any high-severity compliance breach caps the compliance criterion at 0.

## 1. Opening (0 to 15)
- States their first name and "Northwind Bank" (5)
- Gives the recording disclosure at the start of the call (5)
- States the purpose of the call or asks how to help (5)

## 2. Verification (0 to 20)
- Verifies identity with at least two data points (10)
- Completes verification before discussing any account details (10)

## 3. Empathy and tone (0 to 20)
- Acknowledges the customer's situation or feelings in their own words (10)
- Stays respectful and calm; no pressure, sarcasm or dismissive language (10)

## 4. Resolution (0 to 25)
- Understands the need and gives a correct, complete answer or arrangement (10)
- Offers suitable options when the customer can't do what is asked (for example, a payment plan) (10)
- Summarizes what was agreed and gives a reference number (5)

## 5. Compliance (0 to 20)
- Starts at 20. Subtract 5 for each medium breach and 2 for each low breach of the
  compliance checklist. Any high breach sets this criterion to 0.

## Scoring notes
- Taglish and Filipino are acceptable on all calls; score the content, not the language.
- If the customer volunteers sensitive data (such as a full card number), the agent should stop
  them politely and not repeat it. Doing so earns credit under Empathy and tone.
