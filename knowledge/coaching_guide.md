# Coaching guide for team supervisors (sample)

Good coaching points are specific, based on a real moment in the call, and suggest what to say
next time.

## Common themes and suggested coaching
- Empathy under pressure: when a customer shares a hardship (illness, job loss), acknowledge it
  before moving to payment. Example: "I'm sorry to hear about your mother. Let's find an
  arrangement that works for you."
- Verification first: always complete two-point verification before mentioning balances,
  account endings or due dates, even if the customer answers by name.
- Opening script: recording disclosure, name and Northwind Bank, then purpose, in that order.
- Offering options: if the customer can't pay in full, offer a plan before asking for a date.
- Safe payment channels: only the Northwind app, online banking or a branch.
- Strong closing: summarize the agreement, give the reference number, and thank the customer by name.

## What coaching is not
Coaching is developmental. Decisions about performance ratings, warnings or HR action are made
by the team supervisor and HR under the performance management policy, not by QA and not by
the QA agent.
