# Collections and servicing compliance checklist (sample)

Applies to every Northwind Bank call handled by Contoso BPO. This is a fictional training
checklist, not legal advice or an actual regulation.

| Rule | Requirement | Default severity if breached |
|------|-------------|------------------------------|
| CC-01 | Tell the customer the call is recorded, at the start of the call. | Medium |
| CC-02 | Verify identity with at least two data points before discussing any account details, balances or due dates. | High |
| CC-03 | Identify yourself and Northwind Bank, and state the purpose of the call. | Low |
| CC-04 | No threats, harassment or shaming. Never threaten arrest or jail, never threaten to contact the customer's employer, family or neighbors, and never use insulting language. | High |
| CC-05 | When the customer can't pay in full, offer the available options (payment plan, installment, partial payment). | Medium |
| CC-06 | Direct payments only to official Northwind Bank channels: the Northwind app, online banking or a branch. Never to a personal account or e-wallet. | High |
| CC-07 | Close by summarizing what was agreed and giving a reference number. | Low |

## How to report a breach
- Raise one flag per breached rule, quoting the exact line that shows the breach.
- High-severity breaches go to the compliance officer the same day.
- If a rule doesn't apply to the call (for example, CC-05 on a card replacement call), mark it
  "not applicable" rather than "met".
